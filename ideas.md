# Chosen Direction — Signal After Dark

## Design Movement

The site follows a **retro-futurist security operations interface** aesthetic: luminous wireframe infrastructure, data-radar forms, and severe black negative space. The visual language is intentional rather than decorative; 3D forms explain the practice of tracing systems and isolating attack surfaces.

## Core Principles

1. **Depth with restraint:** One rich 3D hero artifact creates the visual signature, while the remainder of the page stays crisp and fast.
2. **Evidence over spectacle:** Neon is used as a state indicator for verification, access paths, and completed research—not as a blanket gradient.
3. **Tactical asymmetry:** Content moves along an offset vertical rail instead of a centered-card grid, creating the feeling of a live case file.
4. **Human signal:** The personal story of a 17-year-old researcher from Itahari remains foregrounded through direct copy and local detail.

## Color Philosophy

The base is nearly black with a blue graphite bias, preserving focus and making the luminance accents feel precise rather than noisy. **Tracer Cyan** is the primary signifier of validated paths and trusted actions. Sparse ultraviolet makes spatial depth visible, while a narrow warning orange appears only for high-importance prompts. This carries the emotional contrast of calm analysis and charged discovery.

## Layout Paradigm

The site is organized around a left-side **signal spine** that travels down the page. Sections attach to it at alternating offsets, creating a scanning flow rather than a conventional centered grid. The hero uses a wide split stage: identity and navigation on the left, a 3D wireframe investigator orbit on the right.

## Signature Elements

1. A three-dimensional wireframe monolith/radar form with orbital nodes and a cyan light trail.
2. Hairline traced-route borders that activate as sections enter the viewport.
3. Compact evidence labels such as `01 / FIELD PROFILE`, `02 / OPERATING PRINCIPLES`, and `03 / CONTACT CHANNEL`.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel instrument-like. Hover states illuminate a single edge, buttons expose an arrow-vector, and navigation gives immediate spatial feedback. Motion is reserved for orientation, reveal, and status; nothing should block reading or manufacture urgency.

## Animation

The hero geometry rotates continuously at a very low velocity with a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback. Nodes pulse asynchronously rather than in a uniform loop. Section traces draw themselves once, and cards lift no more than two pixels. The contact CTA has a subtle cyan field glow only on hover/focus.

## Typography System

**Space Grotesk** is used for body and navigation because it is technical but human. **JetBrains Mono** is used for labels, metadata, and short prompts. Headings use Space Grotesk at a tight, heavy display scale; all metadata is uppercase mono with generous letter spacing. No generic sans-serif default or Inter fallback is used as the visual signature.

## Brand Essence

**Kritik Bhattarai is a Nepal-based, next-generation bug bounty hunter who turns careful observation into responsibly reported security findings.**

Personality: precise, curious, grounded.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are short, factual, and action-aware. CTAs use direct operational language rather than generic conversion phrasing.

Example lines: “Trace the surface. Verify the signal.” and “Have a security lead? Open a secure channel.”

## Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a fractured lowercase `k` assembled from three cyan signal segments inside an open hexagonal scan frame. It carries no text and works as a favicon, profile avatar, and navigation anchor. The wordmark is a custom-spaced rendering of `KRITIK / BHATTARAI`, never a default-font lockup.

## Signature Brand Color

**Tracer Cyan — `#32F5FF`** is the ownable accent and should appear only at moments of confirmed navigation, identity, and contact.

## Style Decisions

Keep page content platform-neutral: no Manus references, badges, or platform attribution appear in the visible website.

Secondary visuals are treated as evidence panels, terrain traces, and documented surfaces rather than additional hero moments. Tracer Cyan remains reserved for identity, verified paths, active navigation, and secure contact; graphite and ultraviolet provide most structural depth. Itahari and Nepal recur through field-node metadata and topographic motifs.
